# Skiff Contributor License Agreement — Individual

**Version 1.1**

Thank you for contributing to Skiff. This agreement sets out the terms under
which your contribution is accepted. It is based on the Apache Software
Foundation's Individual Contributor License Agreement, the most widely used
agreement of its kind, with additions that reflect this project's specific
situation.

Please read it. It is meant to be understandable without a lawyer.

---

## The short version

- **You keep the copyright in everything you write.** Nothing is assigned away.
  You can reuse your own contribution anywhere else, for anything, forever.
- **You give the Project Owner permission to use your contribution**, including
  in versions of Skiff that are not open source and are sold commercially.
- **You confirm the work is actually yours to give** — not your employer's, not
  copied from elsewhere, and not something you lack the right to submit.
- **In return, the Project Owner commits** that contributions accepted into the
  open-source project stay available under the open-source license. This
  agreement adds a permission; it does not take the project closed.

The third point is the one that matters most. If it isn't true, say so before
opening the pull request rather than after.

---

## Why this exists

Skiff is licensed under the **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0**. There is
also a commercial edition planned, which will not be open source.

The AGPL is deliberately strict: code contributed under it cannot be used in a
closed-source product without the contributor's permission. That is a good
property, and it is why Skiff is licensed that way — it stops anyone taking
Skiff, closing it, and selling it.

But it applies to the maintainer too. Without this agreement, a single accepted
pull request would mean the maintainer could never use that code in a commercial
edition without going back and asking you individually. With a hundred
contributors that becomes impossible, and the commercial edition intended to
fund the open-source work quietly stops being viable.

This agreement solves that once, up front, instead of by chasing people down
years later. It is the same arrangement used by MongoDB, Grafana, GitLab,
Sentry and Elastic, among many others.

**What this agreement does not do:** it does not let anyone take your
contribution out of the open-source project. Skiff stays open source under the
AGPL. See section 10, which puts that commitment in the agreement itself rather
than leaving it as a promise.

## How to sign

You do not sign a document or send anything by email.

When you open your first pull request, a bot will comment asking you to confirm.
Reply on that pull request with exactly:

```
I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA
```

Your account identifier, the date, and the pull request are recorded. It is a
one-time step — later pull requests are recognised automatically.

## Questions

If anything here worries you, open an issue and ask before contributing. A
question about the agreement is not an imposition; it is a reasonable thing to
want cleared up before granting permissions. It will not be held against your
pull request.
