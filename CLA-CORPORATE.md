# Skiff Corporate Contributor License Agreement

**Version 1.1**

This agreement is for **companies and other legal entities** whose employees or
contractors contribute to Skiff in the course of their work. It is based on the
Apache Software Foundation's Corporate CLA.

Individuals contributing in their own time, on their own equipment, and outside
the scope of their employment should sign the
[Individual CLA](./CLA.md) instead. Employees of a signing entity still sign the
individual agreement as well; this one covers the entity's rights, that one
covers theirs.

---

## Why this is separate

In most employment arrangements the employer, not the employee, owns code
written on company time or equipment. An individual agreement signed by an
employee therefore may not grant rights the employee does not hold.

This agreement is signed by someone authorised to bind the entity, which
resolves that.

### 5. Representations

The Corporation represents that:

**(a)** It is legally entitled to grant the licenses above.

**(b)** Each Designated Employee is authorised to submit Contributions on its
behalf, and each Contribution submitted by a Designated Employee is either the

**(c)** The Contributions do not, to the best of its knowledge, violate any
third party's intellectual property rights.

**(d)** The Contributions do not knowingly contain any back door, malicious
code, undisclosed telemetry, or intentional security weakness.

**(e)** Contributions containing third-party material are conspicuously marked
as such, identifying the source and any applicable license or restriction.

### 6. Scope

any submitted before the date of signature.

The Corporation shall notify the Project Owner if a Designated Employee ceases
to be authorised. Contributions already submitted remain licensed under this
agreement.

The Project Owner is under no obligation to accept, use, distribute or maintain
any Contribution. Contributions are provided on an "AS IS" basis, without
warranties or conditions of any kind, except as to the representations in
section 5.

### 8. Assignment and successors

The Project Owner may assign this agreement, and the licenses granted under it,
to any successor in interest to the Project, without further consent.

### 9. Term and survival

The licenses granted in sections 2 and 3 are irrevocable and perpetual, and
survive any termination of this agreement.

### 10. Severability and entire agreement

If any provision is held unenforceable, it shall be modified to the minimum
extent necessary to make it enforceable and the remainder shall stand. This
agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Corporation and the
Project Owner concerning the Contributions.

Gujarat, India shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
